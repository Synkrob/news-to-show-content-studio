# News to Show — Workflow Standards (Draft)

This is the small rule set the future workflow agent and video processor will follow.

## Editing standard

The default edit is a light cleanup. It should preserve the creator's natural voice and meaning.

- Accept either a raw recording or a video that has already been edited manually.
- Trim obvious dead space at the beginning and end.
- Do not cut through a word, sentence, or important thought.
- Clean and level the spoken audio.
- Add readable captions and keep them synchronized with the voice.
- Use a simple vertical social format as the first output.
- Do not add an intro, outro, music, logo animation, or visual effects unless requested.
- Do not rewrite the spoken content.
- If the result is uncertain, keep the original clip and send it for review.

## Outdoor visual finishing

Outdoor footage should receive a safe technical correction first: exposure, white balance, contrast, highlights, shadows, and mild noise reduction. The system should avoid aggressive color grading, skin-tone shifts, artificial sharpening, or crushed shadows.

Your preferred look should be captured as a reusable color preset or LUT after reviewing a small set of approved examples. Until that look is proven, the workflow should leave the creative grade for manual review rather than guessing.

If lighting is unusually difficult, the workflow should flag the clip for manual finishing or accept a finished version uploaded by the creator.

## Manual override

Every upload must offer two choices:

1. **Auto-edit my raw video** — apply the standard above.
2. **I already edited this video** — skip processing and use the uploaded version.

## Recommended hybrid mode

The creator may handle the lighting, color, framing, and cuts first, then upload that finished base video. The workflow agent should preserve those visual decisions and handle only the repeatable finishing work:

- Transcribe the speech and add captions.
- Clean and level the audio without changing the delivery.
- Check the beginning and ending for playback problems.
- Create the required platform versions and thumbnails when configured.
- Prepare titles, descriptions, captions, and hashtags for review.

The system should not re-trim, recolor, crop, or add effects to a creator-finished video unless the creator explicitly selects those changes.

## Review standard

The edited file must be checked for a complete first and last frame, audible speech, readable captions, and a playable export. It remains in the **Edited** stage until the creator approves it.

## Publishing standard

- Create platform-specific drafts after the edited video is ready.
- Keep the title, description, caption, and hashtags in plain language.
- Preserve the same story and tone across platforms.
- Never publish automatically without the creator's approval.
- After approval, send the package to the connected scheduler or platform publishing service.

## Daily target

The intended daily routine is: research, select, read the script, upload a raw or finished video, review the result, approve the drafts, and publish.

These are starting defaults. Caption style, video length, platform list, and branding can be adjusted after reviewing a few real videos.
